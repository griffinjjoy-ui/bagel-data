Bagel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Bagel is a college tennis app. It is designed to collect nothing.

No accounts, no tracking, no analytics

Bagel has no sign-up, no login, and includes no analytics or advertising SDKs. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Your favorites stay on your device

Teams and players you follow are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network use

The app ships with its sports data included. When the app checks for updated data, the request contains no personal information — it is a plain download of published sports data (rankings, schedules, results).

About the data shown

Bagel displays publicly available sports information: college and professional tennis rankings, schedules, results, and rosters as published by their sources. Bagel is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the ITA, the NCAA, the WTA or ATP tours, or any school.
