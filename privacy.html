Bagel — Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

Bagel is an independent college-tennis companion app. It is built to need as little of your data as possible.

What the app stores on your device

Your followed teams and players, notification preferences, and display settings are stored locally on your device. They never leave it except as described below.

What leaves your device

If you allow notifications, the app registers your device's anonymous Apple push token together with the list of player IDs you follow and your alert-type preferences, so our relay can send you only the alerts you asked for. This registration contains no name, email, account, or identity of any kind — we could not identify you from it if we tried. It is stored on Cloudflare infrastructure and deleted automatically when Apple reports the token invalid (for example, after you delete the app).

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email collection.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of any data with anyone.
  • No access to contacts, location, photos, or anything else on your device.

Sports data

Scores, rosters, rankings, and results shown in the app are public sporting facts compiled from published sources. Bagel is not affiliated with the ITA, WTA, ATP, or any school.
